Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains how West Capital Lending, Inc. ("we," "us"), NMLS #1566096, doing business on this site as Financing with Alex, collects, uses and protects information submitted through financingwithalex.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us: name, phone number, email, property address, date of birth (optional), estimated credit score, and details about your property and the loan you're looking for.

Information collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, and referring site, collected through cookies and similar tools.

With your authorization only: a consumer credit report obtained through a soft inquiry, which does not affect your credit score.

How we use it

  • To review your situation and contact you about loan options you asked about
  • To prepare and process a loan request, including sharing it with lenders and investors you authorize us to submit your file to
  • To send the text messages and emails you've agreed to receive
  • To meet legal, licensing and regulatory requirements
  • To improve this website

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With lenders, investors, credit bureaus, appraisers and service providers as needed to process a loan request you've made
  • With vendors who help us run our business (such as our CRM and website hosting), who may use it only to provide services to us
  • When required by law, regulation, subpoena or a regulator

Text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How we protect it

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. We do not collect Social Security numbers through this website. If you apply for a loan, you will receive our separate consumer privacy notice as required by the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act.

Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any message to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Cookies: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

California residents

Depending on the information involved, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion or correction, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. Information covered by the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act is governed by that law instead. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children

This site is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
