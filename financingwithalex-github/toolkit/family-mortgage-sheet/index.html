If something happens to me

For whoever is reading this.

  1. Keep making the payment.The loan does not pause. The next payment is still due on the usual date.
  2. Call the servicer and tell them.Ask what they need to confirm you as a "successor in interest." That lets them talk to you about the loan.
  3. Do not rush to sell.In many cases a family member who inherits the home can keep the existing loan. Ask before you decide.
  4. Talk to an estate attorneyabout the title and the estate. That part is legal, not mortgage.

Inherited a home with a mortgage on it?

Who gets the house is for the will and an estate attorney. The loan is Alex's side. Tell him about the home and he'll walk you through your loan options before you decide anything.
